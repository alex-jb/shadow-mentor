orshadow.com — Website Scan

3 independent lenses: design/taste (taste-skill + design-review) · messaging (YC office-hours) · best-in-class benchmark · 2026-07-30
Verdict — the content is procurement-grade; the site is not. All three lenses converged: the words understand the buyer (candid limitations, real evidence-lineage substance, the rare "verification ≠ correctness" nuance) — that's genuinely hard to fake. But the visual layer + information architecture read as a research demo / impressive student project, not a product a bank's model-risk panel would trust. A compliance officer pattern-matches credibility in seconds and this trips the "hobbyist / not production" pattern. The gap is almost entirely craft + positioning, not substance — which means it's fixable fast.

1 · What all three lenses agreed on (fix these first)

#Convergent problemWhy it's fatal for this buyer
1The hero sells the artifact, not the buyer's emergency. Standing headline is an internal tool name — "Shadow Web Audit Room / Local-first audit case workspace · offline browser presentation." The words fair lending, Reg B, ECOA, adverse action, examiner, GDPR, Schufa, independent, reason-code, open-source appear ~0 times above the fold.A fair-lending officer scans 5s for her own pain (the examiner asked "why did the model deny this?"), doesn't find it, leaves. Live craft bugs (visible on screen one)

  • Contrast bug: the hero subtitle "Evidence-grounded AI decision review" computes to pure black over near-black — your primary tagline is the least legible line; fails WCAG AA.
  • Half-built light theme: "Light" mode themes the frame but the hero stays hardcoded dark navy → dark-gray-on-dark-navy subtitle. Only High-Contrast is internally consistent.
  • Layout collision: a "Scroll" label overlaps the "How Shadow Works →" link on the first screen.
  • Unfinished card shipped: the hero "Explore Spatial View" CTA card has an empty description.
The best asset is buried: the printable evidence-lineage report (VERIFIED / NOT EVALUATED / FIRST FAILURE / AFFECTED DOWNSTREAM badges + APPROVAL ABSENT) is the most convincing, procurement-real thing on the site — and it's rendered ~600px, text illegible, mid-scroll. Promote it to a large hero asset.

3 · The rewritten hero (ship tomorrow)

Kill the "Web Audit Room / offline browser presentation" banner. Lead with the buyer's worst day + independence + the one workflow:

H1: "When the examiner asks why your model denied that applicant, answer in an afternoon — not three weeks."

Sub: Shadow is the independent, open-source verifier for automated credit decisions. We didn't build your model — which is exactly why your regulator trusts us to check it. Shadow traces every adverse-action decision to its evidence and reason codes, flags the first step that won't hold up under Reg B / ECOA and GDPR Article 22, and hands you the exam-ready record with a human's sign-off. For fair-lending & model-risk teams, EU and US.

One CTA (repeated, no competitors beside it): "Bring us one real denied application — we'll show you the exam-ready trace in 20 minutes → Book a fair-lending review." Keep the synthetic demo as a tiny secondary link. Reframe the honesty footer from a company disclaimer to a build status: "This is the public synthetic build. Production pilots run on your data under NDA — talk to us."

5 · The 6 changes that move the needle most

  1. Rip out every emoji, replace with one monochrome icon family (Phosphor/Tabler). ~30 min, kills the #1 credibility tell.
  2. Rewrite the hero (§3) — buyer's pain + independence + one workflow + one real value sentence; fix the black-on-black subtitle + the Scroll/link collision + the empty Spatial card; pick one blue, drop the glow.
  3. Add the Open-Source block — GitHub + install + "verify it yourself." (The single most important missing surface.)
  4. Add a conversion CTA + contact — "Book a fair-lending review" / "Request the security brief" + an email.
  5. Promote the printable evidence report to a large, legible hero asset; demote Spatial/Flow to a "Research" footnote.
  6. Rebuild the Security page into a real trust story + a downloadable PDF brief; reframe "not production-ready" as a dated roadmap with a forward path.
